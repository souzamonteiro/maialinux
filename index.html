Pocket Linux for
Handheld Gaming

MaiaLinux turns the Anbernic RG353V into a flexible Linux handheld for gaming, development, experimentation, and customization. We are building a modern, reproducible, community-driven experience that preserves the spirit of ArkOS while preparing the next step toward Debian-based systems and broader device support.

Why MaiaLinux

  • Built around the RG353V platform and its Rockchip ecosystem.
  • Reproducible build flow with release artifacts and checksum verification.
  • Designed to preserve the gaming experience while expanding Linux usability.
  • Prepared for future portability across additional Anbernic handheld targets.

Current project direction

  • Support the proven RG353V base and keep the kernel stack stable.
  • Expand to Anbernic RG DS family support in the next product phase.
  • Explore a Debian-based path, aligning with the broader dArkOS transition.
  • Move toward a cleaner Debian foundation without losing device-specific compatibility.

Roadmap

From stable handheld Linux to broader Debian-native portability.

NOW

RG353V stabilization

Keep the current release stable, reproducible, and validated for real hardware and QEMU-based testing.

NEXT

RG DS support

Adapt the board support layer, kernel configuration, boot scripts, and display handling for the Anbernic RG DS family.

FUTURE

Debian / dArkOS alignment

Evaluate a migration away from the ArkOS-centric base toward a Debian-native foundation, following the wider dArkOS evolution in the ecosystem.
